Job Tracker

A private, single-user automation that keeps track of the owner's own job applications.

What it does
  • Reads job-related emails from the owner's own Gmail inbox.
  • Records application status (applied, interview, rejected, offer) in the owner's private Notion workspace.
  • Sends the owner a notification in their private Discord channel.

This is a personal tool. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the owner uses it.
